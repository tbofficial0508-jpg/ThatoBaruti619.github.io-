About

Where physics
meets intelligence.

I am an aerospace-trained engineer with deep expertise in computational fluid dynamics, turbulence modelling, and scientific machine learning. Currently completing my MSc in CFD at Cranfield, I design and build scientific ML systems in PyTorch: graph-style aerodynamic surrogates, physics-informed models, calibrated validation workflows, and deployment-facing tools.

My work spans the full engineering and ML stack: from CAD geometry and mesh generation through OpenFOAM, UCNS3D, LBM, HPC-style workflows, data extraction, and model training, through to FastAPI/Gradio prototypes and evidence reports. I approach hard problems with scientific rigour and build solutions that show their assumptions, failure modes, and decision value.

My MSc thesis, PiSA, was presented at SAAB UK offices after a four-month SAAB BlueBear-sponsored research project supervised industrially by Vasile Tanasa and academically by Dr Laszlo Konozsy and Dr Dmitry Ignatyev. The work focuses on fast, evidence-supported aero-structural prediction for 3D UAV wing optimisation.

Actively seeking roles across scientific ML, applied AI, CFD engineering, simulation, optimisation, engineering AI platforms, and data-heavy technical teams where trust, speed, and physical credibility matter as much as model score.

ClusterCount-AI4S

A validation-first biomedical image-analysis prototype for automated cell counting in dense and blurred microscopy. It combines a density-map CNN, calibrated patchwise inference, a watershed baseline, uncertainty flags, auditable overlays, Docker packaging, tests, and CI so counts are inspectable rather than black-box.

Built as a reproducible benchmark around clustering, blur, and domain shift, with controlled synthetic validation and external stress testing on fluorescence microscopy from S-BSST265.
